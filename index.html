Stew

The home binder that lives on your phone.

Stew keeps the records for your home in one place — appliances and their manuals, warranties, service providers, bills, and the documents that go with them. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no subscription.

What you can keep

  • Appliances and systems, with model and serial numbers
  • Warranties and receipts, with photos or PDFs attached
  • Service providers — plumber, electrician, HVAC — with tap-to-call
  • Bills and statements, and when the next one is due
  • Mortgage, insurance, property tax, and HOA details

Find it fast

  • Search your records from the iPhone home screen with Spotlight
  • Ask Siri for a provider or a record
  • An Upcoming view gathers due dates and appointments across every home

Add things quickly

  • Share a bill or photo from any app straight into Stew
  • Import details from a photo or a listing using your own AI app
  • Push service and billing dates to your calendar

Back up without thinking

  • Daily snapshots to your iCloud Drive, kept automatically
  • Restore to any recent day, or export a file yourself
  • Keep more than one home

Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Stew stores your data on your device and nowhere else. There are no Stew servers, no accounts, and no sign-in. We never receive, see, or collect anything you put in the app.

What Stew stores, and where

Your records live in a database on your phone. Backups are written to your own iCloud Drive, in a folder that belongs to your Apple account — Apple's privacy policy governs iCloud storage. Deleting the app's data in iOS Settings, or the backup folder in iCloud Drive, removes it; there is no copy anywhere else.

What Stew collects

Nothing. Stew has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data collection of any kind.

Device permissions

Stew asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and uses it only on your device:

  • Photos and camera — attach photos of bills, receipts, and documents to your records
  • Calendar — add billing and service dates to a calendar you choose
  • Contacts — add your service providers to Contacts so Siri and the phone app know them

AI import

Stew can build a text prompt for you to paste into an AI app you already use, so that app can read a bill or listing for you. You choose the app, you send the prompt, and you can read the entire text before sharing it. Whatever you share with that AI app is governed by its privacy policy, not this one. Stew itself sends nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, problems, or requests: open an issue on GitHub. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened, and we’ll get back to you.
